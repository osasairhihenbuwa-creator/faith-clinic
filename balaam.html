A note on Balaam

Numbers 22–25 records Balaam's encounters with Balak and Israel's later unfaithfulness. Numbers 31:16 and Revelation 2:14 connect Balaam with the counsel or teaching that led Israel toward compromise. The resource distinguishes those passages rather than treating the precise counsel as if it were recorded word-for-word in Numbers 22–25.
